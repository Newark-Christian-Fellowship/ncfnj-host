Our Ministries

Join us in a ministry that fits your passion.

Youth Ministry

The NCF youth group welcomes young believers from middle school and high school to gather together. We are learning how to walk the heavenly way, both in our individual lives and as a corporate body. We invite you to join us at our weekly bible studies, our annual winter retreat, and all our other group activities!

Fridays at 7:00 PM

Campus Ministry

NJIT students are invited to join us for Friday night Bible studies and fellowship. We are a welcoming, close-knit community where students can build lasting friendships, grow in their faith, and find a place to belong. Join us as we learn from God's Word, support one another, and build a Christ-centered community.

Fridays at 7:00 PM

Women's Ministry

NCF Women's Ministry is a community where sisters in Christ connect through shared experiences, meaningful conversations, and God's Word. Together, we grow in faith through Bible studies, book studies, and sharing time. Meetings can be online or on site.

Wednesdays at 10:00 AM

Senior Ministry

Experienced members of the faith are invited to gather for worship, fellowship, and spiritual encouragement. Through guest speakers, hymn singing, and meaningful conversation, we grow together in Christ and strengthen one another in faith.

Last Saturday of each month at 10:30 AM

All of our gatherings are bilingual. Choose a service time that matches your preferred language.
