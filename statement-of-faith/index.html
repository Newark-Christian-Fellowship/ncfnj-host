About Us

Statement of Faith

Our core convictions shape who we are.

For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.

John 3:16

01

The Scriptures

We believe the Scriptures are given by God and are the supreme authority in faith and conduct.

02

The Triune God

We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons – the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is the Son of God, born of a virgin, lived sinlessly, died for redemption, rose bodily, and will return.

04

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit dwells in every believer and testifies of Christ.

05

Salvation

We believe redemption is through Christ's blood, and all who believe are born again and become children of God.

06

Man and Sin

We believe man was created in God's image, fell through sin, and lost spiritual life.

NNCF
